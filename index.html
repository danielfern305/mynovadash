The solution

Everything your business
needs in one dashboard

myNovaDash turns your raw freelance data into clear, actionable intelligence — tailored to your specific type of business.

Analytics

Real-time KPI dashboard

Track revenue, expenses, profit margin, effective hourly rate, and more across any custom date range. See your monthly trends at a glance.

AI

Business insights tailored to you

Set up your business profile — type, industry, pricing model — and Claude generates six specific, data-driven insights tailored to your exact business. A plumber gets different advice than a designer.

AI

Trend prediction

Linear regression across your historical data projects what next month's revenue is likely to be. Know whether you're trending up or down before it happens.

Invoices

Invoice tracker with calendar sync

Create invoices, track payment status, and flag overdue amounts. Unpaid invoices automatically appear as projected revenue on their due date. Mark as paid and the calendar updates instantly.

Calendar

Day-by-day cash flow

Log revenue and expenses by individual day. See actual vs projected entries clearly. Invoice projections appear automatically on their due dates so your calendar always reflects what's coming in.

Tax

Tax estimator

Know exactly what you owe each quarter based on your state, filing status, and net profit — before the deadline hits. Shows federal, state, and self-employment tax breakdowns.

Goals

Goal tracking

Set monthly revenue targets and see exactly which months you hit them, with progress bars for every period and streak tracking.

Import

Import external data

Upload any Excel, CSV, PDF, or Word file. Claude reads it, extracts your data, and populates your dashboard automatically. No manual entry required.

Clients

Client breakdown

Track revenue by individual client with pie charts, bar charts, and percentage breakdowns. Instantly see who your most valuable clients are.

Finance

Break-even calculator

Enter your fixed expenses and variable costs. Instantly know the exact revenue you need to break even each month — and your daily target to get there.

Milestones

Achievement notifications

Get notified when you hit your best month ever, reach your monthly goal, or build a consecutive streak. Small moments that make the grind feel real.

Export

PDF reports

Generate a polished, branded PDF with all your KPIs, monthly breakdown, client data, and AI insights — ready to share with an accountant or keep for records.

How it works

Up and running
in three steps

No complicated setup. No accountant required. Just your numbers and a few minutes.

Step 01

Set up your profile

Tell us what type of business you run — freelance designer, independent plumber, marketing consultant, or anything else. This lets the AI tailor every insight to your specific industry.

Step 02

Enter or import your data

Add your monthly totals manually, log day by day on the calendar, or upload an existing spreadsheet and let Claude extract your data automatically.

Step 03

Get insights and plan ahead

Generate AI insights specific to your business, track invoices against your calendar, estimate your tax bill, and see exactly where your business is heading.

Privacy first

Your data stays yours.
Always.

We built myNovaDash with privacy at the core. No selling data, no sharing, no exceptions.

🔒

Your API key never leaves your device

When you use AI features, your Anthropic API key is stored exclusively in your browser. We never see it, never store it on our servers. Every AI request goes directly from your browser to Anthropic.

🚫

We never sell your data

Your financial data is yours. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than providing you the service. No data brokers. No exceptions.

🤖

Not used to train AI

Your business data is never used to train AI models. What you put in stays private — built into how the product works technically, not just as a policy promise.

🏦

Bank-grade data security

All data encrypted in transit and at rest. Row-level database security means your data is only ever accessible by your account — not even us.
